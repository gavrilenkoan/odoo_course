HR Hospital

Doctors, patients, visits & medical history

A compact hospital-management application: manage doctors and their qualifications, patients with medical data, scheduled visits, a hierarchical disease classifier, and a full doctor-assignment history.

Key features

  • Doctors with mentor / intern relations and qualifications
  • Patients with blood group, gender, computed age and insurance number
  • Visits with a status flow: planned, completed, cancelled
  • Hierarchical disease classifier (parent / sub-diseases)
  • Doctor-assignment history synced with the patient's doctor
  • Role-based access: Patient, Intern, Doctor, Manager, Administrator
  • QWeb PDF doctor report and a Kanban board
  • Report wizards and a Ukrainian translation
